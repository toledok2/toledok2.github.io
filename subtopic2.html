The Internet and the Development of Online Learning

Website 1: The Shift to Distance Learning

URL Site Author Abstract
Visit Website Michael K. Barbour This article explores the history of distance learning and how educational opportunities outside traditional classrooms have developed over time. It explains that distance education existed long before modern computers and the internet. Earlier methods included educational films, mailed assignments, radio programs, and instructional television. As technology advanced, schools began using computers, communication networks, and internet-based learning programs. The article also discusses the expansion of online education and the major changes that occurred during the COVID-19 pandemic. This source provides a helpful timeline showing how educational communication and remote learning have evolved over more than a century.

Visit Website UNESCO Global Education Monitoring Report This report examines how digital technology has changed education around the world. It describes the development of educational technology, including the use of computers, internet resources, digital learning platforms, and online educational programs. It explains how students and teachers have gained access to more information and learning opportunities through digital resources. The report also discusses how technology has changed classroom materials, communication, and the way educational institutions organize learning. It provides information about the growth of internet access and the increasing use of digital tools in schools. This source is useful for understanding the transition from traditional educational resources to modern digital learning environments.
