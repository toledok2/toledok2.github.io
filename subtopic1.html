The History of Computers in Education

Website 1: The History of Computers and Their Use in Education

URL Site Author Abstract
Visit Website Harry Singer and Patricia Phelps This publication discusses the development of computers and how they gradually became useful in educational settings. It explains the early history of computing, including the development of large machines and the technological improvements that eventually made smaller computers possible. The authors describe how computers began being used for educational purposes and explore ways they could support teachers and students. The publication is especially useful for understanding why computers were not always easily available in schools. It also provides historical background on how computer technology developed before becoming a more common resource in classrooms.

Website 2: Computers in Education: A Brief History

URL Site Author Abstract
Visit Website Andrew Molnar This article explains how computers became part of education and describes some of the earliest uses of computer technology in schools and universities. It discusses how computers were originally used mainly for mathematics, science, and research before becoming more accessible for general educational purposes. The article also introduces early educational computer programs and explains how technological developments influenced teaching methods. It provides a historical overview of the changes that occurred as computers became more widely available. This source is helpful for comparing early educational technology with the computers and digital resources students use in classrooms today.
