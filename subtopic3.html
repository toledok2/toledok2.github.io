Modern Technology and Artificial Intelligence in Education

Website 1: Guidance for Generative AI in Education and Research

URL Site Author Abstract
Visit Website Fengchun Miao and Wayne Holmes (UNESCO) This publication explores the growing role of artificial intelligence in education and research. It explains how generative AI tools are becoming more available and how they may influence the way students learn and teachers provide instruction. The authors discuss possible educational uses of AI, including helping with learning activities, supporting research, and assisting with curriculum development. The publication also examines the importance of preparing schools and educators for these new technologies. It provides recommendations for using AI responsibly while considering student privacy, age, and educational needs. This source is useful for understanding one of the newest stages in the development of educational technology.

Website 2: Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Teaching and Learning

URL Site Author Abstract
Visit Website U.S. Department of Education, Office of Educational Technology This report examines how artificial intelligence is developing and how it may influence the future of education. It describes ways AI can become part of educational technology and help support teaching, learning, and student development. The report explores how these tools may provide new forms of interaction, assist teachers with educational tasks, and create additional learning opportunities. It also explains the importance of involving educators when introducing AI into classrooms. The publication offers recommendations for schools and educational leaders as they consider how to use these emerging technologies. This source provides insight into how modern technology continues to change education and what future classrooms may look like.
